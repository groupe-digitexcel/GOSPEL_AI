# GOSPEL AI — AI Innovation Constitution

## Purpose

Build a long-lived personal gospel-media system that helps future generations create, learn, preserve and communicate faith messages while keeping human authorship and consent at the center.

## Innovation pillars

### 1. Voice DNA
A private, owner-controlled representation of authorized vocal characteristics.

### 2. Message DNA
A structured representation of message intent, scripture references, audience, language, tone, prayer and closing.

### 3. Scripture Integrity Layer
When scripture is referenced, store the translation/version explicitly and keep generated commentary distinguishable from scripture text.

### 4. Bilingual Bridge
Maintain English and French as first-class languages, with aligned sections rather than simple sentence-by-sentence translation.

### 5. Generation Ledger
Every generated asset should record:
- source text
- language
- voice profile version
- model version
- generation date
- editing operations
- final export checksum

This creates reproducibility and future archival value.

### 6. Human Approval Gate
AI may draft, transform and assemble. The owner approves the final message before publication.

### 7. Legacy Archive
Store approved sermons, prayers, teachings, audio and video with searchable metadata so future generations can discover the material.

### 8. Teaching Copilot
A future assistant can help turn an approved message into:
- sermon outline
- study questions
- youth lesson
- bilingual lesson
- short devotional
- subtitle script
- social-media version

The assistant must distinguish generated material from source material.

### 9. Timeline of Faith
Build a chronological personal ministry archive where messages, projects and testimonies can be linked to dates, themes and scripture references.

### 10. Local-first resilience
Core personal recordings and approved archives should remain exportable and usable without depending permanently on a single AI provider.

## North-star workflow

CAPTURE → UNDERSTAND → VERIFY → CREATE → REVIEW → APPROVE → PUBLISH → ARCHIVE → TEACH → PRESERVE

The system is designed to augment human creativity rather than replace human authorship.

## Future generations

Design every important asset so it can be exported in open formats and understood years later. Avoid locking the archive to one vendor, model or proprietary format.
