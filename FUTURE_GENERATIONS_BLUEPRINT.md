# GOSPEL AI — Future Generations Blueprint

## Generation 1 — Voice

Personal English/French voice generation using authorized reference recordings.

## Generation 2 — Message

AI-assisted sermon and devotional structuring with explicit source tracking.

## Generation 3 — Media

Automated narration, subtitles, scripture cards, visual scenes and MP4 production.

## Generation 4 — Knowledge

Searchable ministry archive connecting themes, scripture, messages, questions and approved teaching material.

## Generation 5 — Teaching

Transform approved content into age-appropriate learning journeys while preserving the distinction between source material and AI-generated teaching aids.

## Generation 6 — Legacy

Create durable, exportable archives so approved material can remain useful even if a particular AI provider, model or hosting service disappears.

## Design rule

Never make the model the owner of the ministry archive.

The human owner remains the authority over recordings, messages, approvals and publication.

## Research frontier

Potential future research areas include:

- multilingual voice adaptation
- low-resource African-language speech
- personal knowledge graphs
- provenance-aware generative media
- local/on-device inference
- efficient model distillation
- semantic sermon indexing
- automatic subtitle alignment
- accessible audio-first education
- long-term digital preservation

Each frontier should be prototyped experimentally and validated before becoming a production feature.
